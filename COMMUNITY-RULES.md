# GG World: community rules

The live world is for people 18 and over. Keep it fun for everyone.

- **Be decent.** No harassment, hate, threats or sexual content.
- **No links, ads, spam or sharing contact details** (yours or anyone
  else's). These are blocked automatically. Each repeat try mutes you for
  longer: 2 minutes, 5 minutes, 1 hour, 1 day, then 1 week. Mutes step back
  down over clean days.
- **No cheating or exploits.** Don't mess with the game or the server.
- **18+ only.**

Admins can mute, kick or ban players who break these rules.

## Report and block

Click a player to open their card:

- **Block** hides their chat and bubbles for you.
- **Report** sends a reason and their recent public chat to us for review.

## Appeals

Think a mute or ban was wrong? Email ken@kenkais.com.
