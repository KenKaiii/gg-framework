# GG Royale: privacy and table rules

Last updated: 26 October 2026

GG Royale is an online blackjack game inside the GG Coder app. It's run by
Ken Kai. Questions or requests: **ken@kenkais.com**.

## No real money

You play with chips you earn by coding and by playing. There is no real
money anywhere in GG Royale: chips, shards, Lucky Deals and rewards can't be
bought, sold, cashed out or traded for anything of value. GG Royale is for
players aged 18 and over.

## What GG Royale keeps

| What | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Your player name | So other players can see who they're playing with | Until you delete your data |
| Game progress: chips, wins, streaks, rewards, locker items, season and bosses | To run the game | Until you delete your data |
| From GG Coder: that a coding run finished, how many commits it made, the kind of bug it fixed (one word, like "test" or "type"), that one of your goals made progress today, and that a deploy happened | To give you chips and bosses for real work | Until you delete your data |
| What you say at a table | To show it to the other players there | The last 20 lines of a table, while the table is open |
| Reports you send about other players | So we can deal with abuse | Until reviewed, then deleted |
| Your IP address | Briefly, to stop too many sign-ups from one place | A rolling one-hour window |

GG Royale **never** receives your code, files, file names, goal names,
prompts, commands, command output, website addresses or anything from your
projects.

## Who else sees it

- Other players see your player name, your table chat, your seat and
  your public game stats (rankings).
- GG Royale runs on Cloudflare (Workers, D1 and Durable Objects), which hosts
  the data for us.
- Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else, and there are no ads or
  trackers.

## Deleting your data

In the game, open **Privacy & credits** and choose **Delete my GG Royale
data**. That permanently deletes your player, chips, progress, rewards and
reports you've sent. GG Coder then forgets its link, so nothing more is
sent unless you link again. You can also email ken@kenkais.com.

## Table rules

- Be kind. No harassment, hate or threats.
- Don't share personal info (yours or anyone else's).
- No ads, spam or links.

You can mute or report any player from the table. Reports are read by a
person, and players who break the rules can be removed.

## GG World

World is a 3D game mode that runs on your computer, inside GG Coder. The other
people in it are simulated for now: you don't meet or talk to real players.

**Online save is optional and off until you agree.** The first time you play,
World asks whether to save online. Until you say yes (and confirm you're 18 or
over), nothing about World leaves your computer.

If you turn it on, World keeps only:

- your World name (if you chose one),
- how your character looks,
- the cosmetic items you own,
- how many coins you've spent,
- your coding level.

Your World name is the same name as your GG Royale player name (one name per
player). Changing it in World changes it in GG Royale too.

The online save runs on Cloudflare (Workers and D1), the same service GG Royale
uses. It's linked to this computer the same way GG Royale is: a private key
GG Coder keeps on your computer.

It never gets your code, files, prompts or project names. It's kept so your
save survives reinstalls and carries over to your other computers, and it's
kept until you delete it.

To delete it: in World, open the cloud key next to Back and choose **Delete my
online save**, or email ken@kenkais.com. Deleting your GG Royale data also
deletes your World save.

## Changes

If this notice changes in a way that matters, GG Coder shows you the
GG Royale link box again before you play.
